The Lantern Table

Every decision on the Make the Murlan table look full and feel alive map, on one table at a phone's 874×402: the lamp that follows whoever is on move, worsted twill in a walnut rail with a brass line, plastic cards that catch the lamp, the particles for each moment and the sounds picked by ear. It also shows what this round adds: the score pill at the top right, which opens on a tap and opens itself when a manche ends; the turn pill at the top centre; and the menu and reactions on the left edge. Every decision, and where it shows

DecisionOn this table
Art directionThe lantern, evolved. The lamp's pool moves to the seat on move and sways, never twice the same. A carved rim frames the table and the right band stays empty. Seat discs, badges, the turn-clock arc, the PASSO chip, the beaten combination at −7° and +9, and the red last-card badge are today's.
Cloth and railWorsted twill, fine threads, weave visible, no hem. Drawn thread by thread by one lamp-lit shader. Walnut rail with a brass line, in today's 14 pt band.
CardsPlastic, lamp reflected: a soft spot of the lamp slides over the cards nearest it, with a faint streak. Faces and court art are today's.
Felts and backsVerde and Smeraldo stay the defaults. The other felts and backs take the proposed colours. Try them under the table.
ParticlesOne per moment, all under 200 live: specks on the deal, dust on landing, an ember along the rim on the hand-off, the bomb, 46 motes on a win and the rain for a partita, the lamp turning away on a loss, grey on a dropped connection, and motes and a moth at rest.
SoundFourteen picks by ear, listed under What you hear. Each plays on its action.
End of a mancheThe score pill, always at the top right, showing your points toward 21 and your place. Tap it and it opens. When a manche ends it opens by itself: each seat's points count in, the rows re-rank, and it closes before the next deal starts at 2500 ms.
End of a partitaThe open pill becomes today's board, on the felt, over the dimmed table. It settles within 4000 ms and waits for a tap. A tap during the ending skips to the settled state.
LayoutThe turn pill sits at the top centre. The menu is at the top left and the reactions at the bottom left, whichever side the phone's cutout is on. Today both follow the cutout, so on a phone turned the other way they sit on the right.
DroppedThe chalk slate from the art direction, and its chalk sounds. The score lives in the pill.
