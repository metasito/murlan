Exchange Variants

Four ways to show the card exchange, each built only from things the table already has: the turn pill, the pile and its label, the PASSO chip, the ember on the rim and the lamp. In every variant both cards cross the table face up, so every seat sees them move. The name tags that rode the cards are gone. Choose a variant, then a case, and compare. You can slow it down, pause it or scrub it, and the timings are sliders at the bottom.
