Lantern Table Playthrough

Table art direction · Direction A, second pass

The lantern, evolved: three tricks played out

Four bots play three tricks, a bomb, a player down to their last card and a player going out, then it loops. The lamp now follows whose turn it is, and card shadows fall away from it. Seats, passes and the pile follow today's components. Pause it, step through it, or pick any move from the list to jump there.

The moves

    Matched to today's table

    • The seat is a 33 pt dark disc with a gold rule and the player's initial. The card count sits in its own badge at the disc's foot, outside it.
    • The turn clock is an arc sweeping the rim of the seat whose turn it is. On your turn the chip counts down instead.
    • A pass shows a PASSO chip on that seat. Three passes in a row close the trick, and the cards sweep to the winner at 60% scale.
    • The beaten combination lies under the new one, turned −7° and dimmed. The combination's name sits under the pile.
    • At one card left, the badge grows 1.25× and turns red.

    New in this direction

    • The lamp leans toward the seat on move and keeps a slow sway, so you can tell whose turn it is from across the room.
    • Card, back and slate shadows point away from the lamp as it moves.
    • Micro dust on every landing, bigger on the bomb. Motes in the cone, and a moth every 12 s.
    • The bomb: a lamp flare, a hard swing, gold sparks at three sizes, chalk dust off the slate, and a shake.
    • Going out: gold motes burst from the seat, a trophy replaces the badge, and three fresh chalk strokes are drawn on the slate.

    Removed after your review

    The manche and partita plaque, the "verso 21 · manche 6" header on the slate, and the candle. The right band stays empty on purpose: the moving lamp light and the dust are what fill it.
