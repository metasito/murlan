SETTLED BY THE RESEARCH
Not up for a vote — these came out of the sources
Long press, 500ms, then move
A press already selects and a press on GIOCA plays. A distance threshold is worse than nothing here — the hand is a horizontal fan, so moving a finger across it is what reading your hand looks like. 500ms is the gesture library's own default.
Held is not a bigger selected
Selection already owns lift, rotation and border. What is left to held is scale, shadow, and the one nothing else on the table does: leaving the arc and following the finger in free two dimensions.
A gap, never a ghost
The layout already computes each card's place from its index, so opening a gap is not an effect to add — it is what the fan does when the index changes. A ghost placeholder would need geometry the arc has not got.
No sort button, and still Level AA
WCAG 2.5.7 needs a way to reorder without dragging. Slider and ReplayControls already answer that shape with accessibility actions — invisible to sighted players. Nothing is added to the screen.
WHAT I AM ASKING YOU
Three chips sit above the prototype. Drag a card with each setting and pick what it should ship as.
1
How far the card leaves the fan
Off the fan keeps it close to where it came from and reads as picking a card out of a held hand. In the air is unmistakable but the card stops belonging to the hand while it moves.
2
How wide the gap opens
Overlap step keeps the hand the same width the whole time, so nothing else moves under your thumb. Full card cannot be missed, but the whole fan breathes in and out as you drag.
3
What release looks like
Settle is quiet and quick. Bounce is the one small overshoot the design system already wrote a spring for — more physical, and one more thing happening every time you tidy your hand.
