Two of the 33 are not timings at all. showNotification({ duration }) is how long a banner stays readable — a reading budget, not motion. It belongs on its own dwell group whatever this ticket decides; folding a 4.5 s read in with a 90 ms flash is a category error, and seven call sites do it.
One is deliberately off-scale. sparkOffset scatters sixteen sparks with 60 + (i % 5) * 22. The point is that they are not synchronised, so those two numbers are chosen against each other and belong as named constants beside the function, not as steps everything else shares.
