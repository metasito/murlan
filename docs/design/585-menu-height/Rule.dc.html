#585 — THE RULE, NOT SIX COPIES OF A NUMBER
A menu screen names the region that takes the slack
The defect was never a missing height calculation
Every screen already gets the full window: MenuLayout's content box carries flexGrow: 1. What no screen says is which of its regions is elastic, so all of them stack at the top and the slack lands at the bottom in one lump. Nothing here measures a window — measuring is what produced six copies last time.
One primitive, in the shared layout
MenuCard grow for a card that should reach the floor, and an exported takesSlack style for a plain region. Both are flexGrow: 1; flexShrink: 0; flexBasis: auto — it grows into spare height and never gives any back, which is what keeps it correct inside the outer ScrollView when the content is taller than the window.
Which region, per screen
/(online) — nothing grows. Two short forms; the columns centre in the height the row already stretches to, and the divider finally spans a composition rather than a void.
/leaderboard — the card reaches the floor, the board inside it is elastic, the note and Indietro sit on the card's floor.
/friends — the two list sections share the slack 2:1; each empty state centres in its own band.
in-game settings sheet — the rows keep their rhythm and centre in the region that was already elastic.
What the check pins
tests/e2e/menuHeight.spec.ts opens each screen at a phone height and a tablet height and compares where its content ends. A screen has to absorb a stated share of the extra height — and the share is per screen, because centring a short form is a legitimate answer and stretching one is not. A screen whose content already overflows the tall window scrolls and is skipped: it has no void.
Two things the audit's table got wrong, and the measurement says so
/(online)/room already passes at 390→834 and 844→1112: its seat list is elastic. The audit read an empty room.
/rules and /profile are 1885px and 1696px of content — taller than any window here. Identical bottoms there mean "the same content", not "a void". /rules' scrolling is #587.
To tweak
The 2:1 split on /friends, whether /(online)'s columns centre or its cards grow, and whether the settings rows centre or spread evenly — those are the three judgement calls. Say which you'd rather and it is a one-line change each.
