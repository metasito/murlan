**Goal:** No seat and no pile moves when a hand shrinks or empties (#13). The lamp lights the seat on move at every phone, and a player can tell which seat that is (#12). The seat on move shows its countdown ring offline even when it leads a round (#14, research item 12).

**Architecture:**
- **Bands.** The top band and the side slots are sized for the fan's drawn cap (`FAN_DRAWN_CARDS`), never for a count. `topBandHeight(scale)` and `sideSlotHeight(scale)` move to `components/tableFrame.ts` and are the only sizes; they read the fan's cap-sized extent from one count-free function in plan 2's `components/fanGeometry.ts`, `drawnFanBounds(scale)`. `GameTable` sets them on the sections, and `components/flightPhysics.ts` derives the pile and every seat anchor from them through one new function, `anchorPoints(geometry)`.
- **Aim.** The lamp aims at those anchors. `lampPools(anchors, width, height)` in `components/table/lampRig.ts` replaces the fixed `POOL`. `GameTable` computes the anchors from the frame it already lays out (`anchorPoints(tableGeometry(seatGeometry))`), so the lamp and the table cannot drift apart.
- **Symmetry (the owner's review of the decisions page).** Every seat's light has the same shape round that seat:
  - the light sits straight inside the seat, with no sideways tilt;
  - left and right are mirror images;
  - the top is as far inside as the hand.

  The light's size (the pool and the dark falloff) scales with how far the seat on move is from its nearest other seat. That size is a third component of every pool, `reach`, and the rig glides it with the light. The result is that the seat on move stands out by about the same ratio at every seat.
- **Vignette (owner decision D1: the light follows the player).** The vignette is centred on the light point, and the pool and the falloff take the owner's tuned sizes (`uPoolR`, `uVigR`) times `reach`. Two variants ship in the diagnostics build, the owner's A and a wider B, and both meet the symmetry bound.
- **Guards.** A compiler-API scan refuses a count in the signature of any seat, band or pile function, chosen by module, and refuses any call from one into the fan's shape that takes a count. The Playwright checks measure what the player sees: ring and pile centres, the lamp against the measured rings, and the Skia felt's own pixels.

## For plan 4: the lamp's reach redraws the felt

Plan 4 decides when the felt redraws. Task 5 gives the light a size that moves, so plan 4 must redraw on it. The names, exactly:
- The shared value is still `rig.lamp: SharedValue<Lamp>` from `useLampRig` (`components/table/useLampRig.ts`).
- `Lamp.r` is the drawn reach, glided each frame by `stepLamp` at the pool's rate (`GLIDE`). `Lamp.tr` is its target, set by `lampControls.setTarget(s, [x, y, reach], reduced)`. `Lamp.drawn.r` is the reach last drawn.
- `lampMoved` (Task 5, Step 4b) counts the lamp still only when `Math.abs(s.r - s.drawn.r) <= REACH_STEP`, with `REACH_STEP = POINT_STEP / 600`, and writes `drawn.r` when it redraws.
- The Skia felt reads the reach in `FeltCanvas`'s `uniforms` `useDerivedValue`: `lightUniforms(light, lamp.value.r)` gives the shader's `uPoolR` and `uVigR` (Task 7). The web fallback bakes from `pool[2]` (`feltFallback.tsx`).
- A redraw gate that watches only `lx`, `ly`, `f` and `level` misses a reach glide: when the turn passes from the hand to a side seat, the light's size changes by about 1.4× over the glide.

## Global Constraints

- **Layout tolerance.** Seat ring centres, the top ring and the pile centre stay within **0.5 pt** of the deal position (`counts-13-13-13`). This holds for **13/13/13, 2/13/2, 2/1/2 and top-out**, at every `PHONES` entry (`tests/e2e/helpers/phones.ts`: 568×320, 844×390, 874×402, 956×440).
- **Guarded signatures.** The guarded set is chosen by module: every exported function of `components/seatLayout.ts` and `components/tableFrame.ts`, and the position functions of `components/flightPhysics.ts`. A guarded function takes only geometry. Every number in its parameters, to depth 4, has a name from the pinned vocabulary `GEOMETRY` or `SEAT` (Task 2), and none sits under a property named for a count. No `hand` or `handCount` appears. Every call it makes into `components/fanGeometry.ts`, directly or through a helper, must be to a function that passes the same rule. The layering is `fanGeometry.ts → seatLayout.ts`, never the reverse; `tableFrame.ts` sits above both. The guard must flag the planted probes.
- **Legibility is read off pixels.** Sample the annulus **17–45 pt** round each ring on the **Skia** felt, pinned with `skiaOnSoftware(page)`. The check is on-move ÷ brightest-other ≥ the floor. The floor is the owner-picked variant's device-measured ratio **minus 10 %**. Until then CI uses **3.8** (Task 6 says why).
- **Evenness.** For each phone, the worst of the four seats' on-move ratios ÷ the best is **≥ 0.8** (`LAMP_SYMMETRY`). It is checked in the pixels (`lampLegibility.spec.ts`), in the shader rendered by CanvasKit (`lampShape.test.ts`), and on the device for both variants (bench `lamp-variants`).
- **Shape.** Each light sits straight inside its seat: no sideways offset at the top or bottom, and no vertical offset at the sides. It sits the same distance in, per unit of `reach`, at all four seats. Left and right mirror each other.

## Review Focus

1. **The window changes mid-game** (rotation, iPad split view). The bands recompute from `scale`, and the lamp re-aims because `pools` depends on `W`, `H` and the insets. Pinned by Task 5's "re-aims when the window changes" test.
2. **Two and three players.** A table with no left seat must still aim at the seats that exist. `reach` reads only the top and the hand, which every table has, so an absent seat never changes it. Pinned by Task 5's `feltParityGrid.ts` change, which walks 2, 3 and 4 players with measured anchors.
3. **The start announcement.** The lamp swings to the table centre and back to the seat. Pinned by Task 5's `startAnnouncement.spec.ts` change.
4. **The viewer's own turn, with the hand lifted.** The bottom pool aims at the *unlifted* hand zone, so the light does not jump as the hand rises. Pinned by `lampSeats` `lamp-bottom`, whose anchor ignores the transform (`offsetTop`).
5. **The sway.** Without reduced motion the pool swings ±20 pt, and the worst frame is what a player sees. CI measures at rest, so Task 8's device gate takes the minimum over a sway period.
6. **The light moving between seats.** Its size changes from the hand's to a side seat's. `reach` glides at the pool's own rate, so it never jumps. Pinned by `lampRig.test.ts` "the reach glides with the light".

function counted(checker: ts.TypeChecker, type: ts.Type, where: string, depth: number): string[] {
  if (depth > 4) return [];
  if (type.isUnionOrIntersection()) return type.types.flatMap((t) => counted(checker, t, where, depth));
  if (type.flags & ts.TypeFlags.NumberLike) {
    const segments = where.split(".");
    return ALLOWED.has(segments.at(-1)!) && !segments.slice(0, -1).some((s) => /count/i.test(s)) ? [] : [where];
  }
  if (checker.isArrayType(type) || checker.isTupleType(type)) {
    return checker.getTypeArguments(type as ts.TypeReference).flatMap((t) => counted(checker, t, `${where}[]`, depth + 1));
  }
  if (!(type.flags & ts.TypeFlags.Object) || type.getCallSignatures().length > 0) return [];
  return type.getProperties().flatMap((p) => {
    const inner = `${where}.${p.name}`;
    if (p.name === "hand" || p.name === "handCount") return [inner];
    if (p.declarations?.every((d) => d.getSourceFile().isDeclarationFile)) return [];
    return counted(checker, checker.getTypeOfSymbol(p), inner, depth + 1);
  });
}

const resolve = (checker: ts.TypeChecker, s: ts.Symbol) => (s.flags & ts.SymbolFlags.Alias ? checker.getAliasedSymbol(s) : s);

function takesCount(checker: ts.TypeChecker, fn: ts.Symbol): string[] {
  const params = checker.getTypeOfSymbol(fn).getCallSignatures().flatMap((s) => s.getParameters());
  return params.flatMap((p) => counted(checker, checker.getTypeOfSymbol(p), p.name, 0));
}

function reachesFan(checker: ts.TypeChecker, node: ts.Node, seen: Set<ts.Node>): string[] {
  if (seen.has(node)) return [];
  seen.add(node);
  const found: string[] = [];
  const visit = (n: ts.Node): void => {
    const callee = ts.isCallExpression(n) ? checker.getSymbolAtLocation(n.expression) : undefined;
    const fn = callee && resolve(checker, callee);
    const body = fn?.valueDeclaration;
    if (fn && body && path.resolve(body.getSourceFile().fileName) === FAN) {
      found.push(...takesCount(checker, fn).map((w) => `${fn.name}(${w})`));
    } else if (body && !body.getSourceFile().isDeclarationFile) {
      found.push(...reachesFan(checker, body, seen));
    }
    ts.forEachChild(n, visit);
  };
  visit(node);
  return found;
}

function scan(files: string[], guarded: (file: string, name: string) => boolean, probe?: string): Map<string, string[]> {
  const host = ts.createCompilerHost(options);
  const read = host.getSourceFile;
  host.getSourceFile = (file, ...rest) =>
    probe && path.resolve(file) === PROBE ? ts.createSourceFile(file, probe, ts.ScriptTarget.Latest) : read(file, ...rest);
  const program = ts.createProgram(files, options, host);
  const checker = program.getTypeChecker();
  const found = new Map<string, string[]>();
  for (const file of files) {
    for (const exported of checker.getExportsOfModule(checker.getSymbolAtLocation(program.getSourceFile(file)!)!)) {
      const fn = resolve(checker, exported);
      if (!guarded(file, exported.name) || !fn.valueDeclaration) continue;
      if (checker.getTypeOfSymbol(fn).getCallSignatures().length === 0) continue;
      found.set(exported.name, [...takesCount(checker, fn), ...reachesFan(checker, fn.valueDeclaration, new Set())]);
    }
  }
  return found;
}

  dir: FlyDirection;

  tableLeft: number;
  tableRight: number;
  tableTop: number;
  /** `TableFrame.surplus` — how far above the window's bottom the table ends. */
  surplus: number;
  /** HAND_ZONE_H(handCardH, bottomPad) — the hand row's own height. */
  handZoneH: number;
}

/** The table as laid out, with no seat, count or hand in it. */
export type TableGeometry = Omit<FlightOriginInput, "dir">;

type WindowPoint = { x: number; y: number };

/** Where the pile's centre lands, and the band the side seats share with it. */
function pileGeometry(input: TableGeometry): { centerX: number; centerY: number; tableFloor: number; midH: number } {
  const topSectionH = topBandHeight(input.scale);
  const tableFloor = input.windowHeight - input.surplus;
  const midH = tableFloor - input.tableTop - topSectionH - input.handZoneH;
  return {
    centerX: input.tableLeft + (input.windowWidth - input.tableLeft - input.tableRight) / 2,
    centerY: input.tableTop + topSectionH + midH / 2,
    tableFloor,
    midH,
  };
}

/**

 * before its lift. The bands are fixed (`topBandHeight`, `sideSlotHeight`), so none of these moves with a hand.
 */
export function anchorPoints(input: TableGeometry): Record<FlyDirection | "pile", WindowPoint> {
  const ring = SEAT_DISC * input.scale;
  const { centerX, centerY, tableFloor, midH } = pileGeometry(input);
  const sideY = centerY - midH / 2 + sideSlotHeight(input.scale) / 2;
  return {
    pile: { x: centerX, y: centerY },
    bottom: { x: centerX, y: tableFloor - input.handZoneH / 2 },
    top: { x: centerX, y: input.tableTop + seatLabelH(input.scale) + ring / 2 },
    left: { x: input.tableLeft + Spacing.sm + ring / 2, y: sideY },
    right: { x: input.windowWidth - input.tableRight - Spacing.sm - ring / 2, y: sideY },

}

const RING_TOLERANCE = 0.5;

function seatAnchors(rows) {
  const states = [];
  for (const r of rows) {

/** A seat's point as the page laid it out: its ring's centre, or the hand zone's before its lift (`offsetTop` ignores the transform). */
export async function seatAnchor(page: Page, side: "top" | "left" | "right" | "bottom"): Promise<{ x: number; y: number }> {
  return page.evaluate((side) => {
    if (side === "bottom") {
      const hand = document.querySelector<HTMLElement>('[data-testid="hand-zone"]');
      if (!hand) throw new Error("no hand zone on the table");
      let x = 0;
      let y = 0;
      for (let e: HTMLElement | null = hand; e; e = e.offsetParent as HTMLElement | null) {
        x += e.offsetLeft;
        y += e.offsetTop;
      }
      return { x: x + hand.offsetWidth / 2, y: y + hand.offsetHeight / 2 };
    }
    const seat = side === "top" ? "top-seat" : `side-seat-${side}`;
    const r = document.querySelector(`[data-testid="${seat}"] [data-testid="seat-ring"]`)?.getBoundingClientRect();
    if (!r) throw new Error(`no ${side} ring on the table`);
    return { x: r.x + r.width / 2, y: r.y + r.height / 2 };
  }, side);
}

/** Where a pool is in design points, and how big its light is against the page the owner tuned it on. */
export type Pool = readonly [x: number, y: number, reach: number];
type Point = { x: number; y: number };

/** The rail ring's own centre, where the start announcement's words are, at the tuned size. */
export const TABLE_CENTRE: Pool = [465, 201, 1];

/**
 * The decisions page the owner tuned the light on (#1259): its hand and top ring 269 pt apart, and
 * "your pool height" 380 — the light 7 pt inside the hand, once `LIGHT_ABOVE` is taken off.
 */
const TUNED = { near: 269, inward: 7 } as const;
const INWARD: Record<FlyDirection, readonly [number, number]> = { bottom: [0, -1], top: [0, 1], left: [1, 0], right: [-1, 0] };

/**
 * Every seat's light the same shape round it: straight in from the seat, sized by how far the nearest
 * other seat is, so the seat on move stands out alike at each. The top and the hand are on every table.
 */
export function poolOver(side: FlyDirection, seat: Point, top: Point, bottom: Point, width: number, height: number): Pool {
  const { sx, sy } = designScale(width, height);
  const apart = (a: Point, b: Point) => Math.hypot((a.x - b.x) / sx, (a.y - b.y) / sy);
  const near = side === "top" || side === "bottom" ? apart(top, bottom) : Math.min(apart(seat, top), apart(seat, bottom));
  const reach = near / TUNED.near;
  const [ix, iy] = INWARD[side];
  return [seat.x / sx + ix * TUNED.inward * reach, seat.y / sy + iy * TUNED.inward * reach + LIGHT_ABOVE, reach];
}

export function lampPools(anchors: Record<FlyDirection, Point>, width: number, height: number): Record<LampTarget, Pool> {
  const at = (side: FlyDirection) => poolOver(side, anchors[side], anchors.top, anchors.bottom, width, height);
  return { bottom: at("bottom"), top: at("top"), left: at("left"), right: at("right"), centre: TABLE_CENTRE };
}

// lib/diagnostics/lampLegibility.ts — how much the seat on move out-lights the others, read off the felt's
// own pixels: tests/e2e/lampLegibility.spec.ts on the web, the lamp-variants bench scenario on the device.

export const ANNULUS = { inner: 17, outer: 45 } as const;

/** The floor until the owner's device pick sets it (plan 3, Task 9): half the weaker variant's modelled worst seat. */
export const LAMP_FLOOR = 3.8;

/** The owner's "about equal for each player": the seat that stands out least, over the one that stands out most. */
export const LAMP_SYMMETRY = 0.8;

export interface Pixels {
  data: ArrayLike<number>;
  width: number;
  height: number;
}

const linear = (c: number) => {
  const s = c / 255;
  return s <= 0.04045 ? s / 12.92 : ((s + 0.055) / 1.055) ** 2.4;
};

export function annulusLuminance(px: Pixels, at: { x: number; y: number }, perPt: number): { mean: number; count: number } {
  const r0 = ANNULUS.inner * perPt;
  const r1 = ANNULUS.outer * perPt;
  let sum = 0;
  let count = 0;
  for (let y = Math.max(0, Math.floor(at.y - r1)); y < Math.min(px.height, Math.ceil(at.y + r1)); y++) {
    for (let x = Math.max(0, Math.floor(at.x - r1)); x < Math.min(px.width, Math.ceil(at.x + r1)); x++) {
      const d = Math.hypot(x + 0.5 - at.x, y + 0.5 - at.y);
      const i = (y * px.width + x) * 4;
      if (d < r0 || d > r1 || px.data[i + 3] === 0) continue;
      sum += 0.2126 * linear(px.data[i]) + 0.7152 * linear(px.data[i + 1]) + 0.0722 * linear(px.data[i + 2]);
      count++;
    }
  }
  return { mean: count ? sum / count : 0, count };
}

export function legibility(means: Record<string, number>, onMove: string): number {
  return means[onMove] / Math.max(...Object.entries(means).filter(([k]) => k !== onMove).map(([, v]) => v));
}

export function evenness(ratios: readonly number[]): number {
  return Math.min(...ratios) / Math.max(...ratios);
}

export async function departMockup(page: Page): Promise<void> {
  await page.addInitScript(
    ({ swaps, light }) => {
      const w = window as unknown as { __departed: number; __lampReach?: () => number };
      w.__departed = 0;
      const source = WebGLRenderingContext.prototype.shaderSource;
      WebGLRenderingContext.prototype.shaderSource = function (shader, src) {

          w.__departed++;
          src = swaps.reduce((s, [from, to]) => s.replace(from, to), src.replace("vec3 stops(", "uniform float uPoolR,uVigR;\nvec3 stops("));
        }
        source.call(this, shader, src);

      const draw = WebGLRenderingContext.prototype.drawArrays;

## Design questions for the lead

1. **"`useLampRig` is fed the inset-aware table frame."**
   - Assumed: the *aim* is fed the frame. `GameTable` builds the anchors from its own `seatGeometry` (`anchorPoints(tableGeometry(seatGeometry))`), the frame it lays the seats out in, so the lamp and the table cannot drift.    - The aim offsets are in design points and stretch with the felt.
2. **How the symmetry requirement is met: a light whose size scales per seat.**
   - Every light sits `7·reach` straight inside its seat. The top and bottom use the vertical, the sides the horizontal, with no tilt. The 7 is youY 380 − 40 − the page's hand at 347.
   - The same shape at a fixed size cannot be even: 0.22, because the table is twice as wide as tall.
   - A fixed per-axis offset (the sides 80 pt in) tunes to 0.91 on one frame but gives 0.54 on another.
   - So the pool and the falloff both scale with `reach` = the seat on move's nearest-other distance ÷ 269, the distance on the page he tuned. This gives 0.88–0.98 on every frame, for both variants.
   - `reach` rides the rig as a third pool component and glides at the pool's rate. Plan 4 must keep it in whatever decides a felt redraw.
   - The mockup's pools (the discs plus [0,39] and [±119,17]) are no longer the app's. Parity now aims the mockup through the app's own `lampPools` (`MOCKUP_POOLS`).
3. **The guard, as the lead specified it, and the two places it had to differ.** The set is chosen by module, not by name, as asked: every export of `seatLayout.ts` and `tableFrame.ts`, and `anchorPoints`, `flightOrigin`, `seatPoint` and `tableGeometry` in `flightPhysics.ts`. The probes include `n`, `cards` and a `fanGeometry` import, as asked.
   - **"No numeric parameter other than `scale`" would fail about a dozen legitimate exports:**
     - sizes: `HAND_ZONE_H(cardH, bottomPad)`, `handRowHeadroom(cardH)`, `handVisibleH(cardH)`, `exchangeArrivalRise(cardH, bottomPad, rowRise)`, `surplusHeight(width, height, scale)`, `railWidth(leftInset, scale)`, `computeTableFrame({ width, height, insets, scale })`, `computeScreenPads`, `notificationTopOffset({ topPad, … })`;
     - seat identity: `seatDirection(seat, viewerSeat, playerCount)`, `getOpponentPosition(steps, total)`, `arrangeOpponents`, `viewerOwnsSeat`;
     - the fields of `TableGeometry`/`SeatPlace` themselves.

     So the rule is a pinned vocabulary of quantities, `GEOMETRY` plus `SEAT`, with no count-named parent. Its limit is stated in the test: a type cannot tell a count named `width` from a width, and adding a name is a reviewed diff.
   - **The layering is as the lead set it, and checked:** `fanGeometry.ts` imports `seatLayout.ts` (`FAN_DRAWN_CARDS`, `SEAT_DISC`, `seatGap`), and `seatLayout.ts` never imports `fanGeometry.ts`.    - **But "no guarded module imports fanGeometry" cannot hold for `tableFrame.ts`:** the band *is* the fan at its cap (§3), so whatever sizes the band must learn the cap fan's extent from `seatFanArc`.      - `tableFrame.ts`, above both, imports `fanGeometry.ts` for one count-free function, `drawnFanBounds(scale)`;
     - a guarded function may reach `fanGeometry.ts`, directly or through any helper, only through a function that itself passes the signature rule. `seatFanArc` and `fanPoint` take counts and are refused;

   - If plan 2 Task 9 has not landed, `exchangeFlight` is unguarded. It loses its counts here anyway.
