## 1. Goal and what the owner sees

- **#13:** no seat ring and not the pile moves as hands shrink or a seat goes out. The research
  measured the side ring falling 180.2 → 162.3 pt at two cards and → 111.8 pt with the top seat
  out, and the pile 220.5 → 193.4 pt, at 874×402.
- **#12:** the lamp lights the seat on move on every phone, and a player can tell which seat that
  is. Today the lamp aims at fixed design constants mapped by the window, so "you" is lit above
  the hand and the side lights miss the rings at the iPhone SE; the vignette sits at (457, 210)
  whatever the lamp does, so a side seat on move reads about 1× the brightest other seat.
- **#14, item 12 (D4):** the seat on move shows its countdown ring offline even when it leads a
  round. Today a bot-led round is dark.
- **D4 #9 (from plan 5):** under device insets the light sits on the pile, not about 14 pt off it.

### Fixed by the design (§3, §4, §8; not owner decisions, not reopened here)

- A seat's place is never a function of a card count: the top band and side slots are sized for
  the drawn cap (`FAN_DRAWN_CARDS`), the top band keeps that height when the top seat is out, the
  side ring centres in its fixed slot, and the sizes live in `components/tableFrame.ts`. A
  compiler-API scan guards the class; a Playwright spec holds rings and pile within 0.5 pt of the
  deal at every `PHONES` entry (`tests/e2e/helpers/phones.ts`).
- The lamp aims at the ring centres the inset-aware frame laid out; the vignette follows the light.
- Legibility is read off the Skia felt's pixels in the annulus 17–45 design pt round each ring. The floor
  comes from the device: two tuned variants, the owner picks one by eye, floor = its ratio − 10 %.
- The diagnostics compile in only with `EXPO_PUBLIC_DIAGNOSTICS=1`.

## Decided 2026-09-29

The owner's rule: "make it look and feel good, that's all I care. as close as possible to
mockup." Only what a player sees goes to him, by eye, at a gate.

Settled by eye at **G1**, 2026-09-29, on the mockup
(https://claude.ai/artifact/4DsgAgoodmgp2Um2Ez711t, the shipped shader with Task 8's two lines, drawn
with WebGL; sliders left at 380 · 380 · 420 · 380). Each proposal stands as written:
- **Q1, the shape of "about equal":** every light straight inside its seat (no tilt, sides
  mirrored), pool and falloff scaled by a per-seat `reach` = nearest-other-seat distance ÷ 269 (his
  page's hand-to-top). Modelled evenness 0.88–0.98, against 0.51 for his page as tuned. Owner:
  "straight in, the same round each seat: yes".
- **Q2, variant B:** 420/420, a step wider than his 380/380 (A 11.9–13.3×, B 7.7–8.5×). Owner: "no
  difference" between A and B by eye. Superseded below: that was his pick, and there is no B.
- **Q4, "your pool height 380":** 7·reach inside the hand, exactly [443, 380] on his page. Owner:
  "right".
- **Q6, gloss and motes:** #1260's gloss falloff and #1261's motes' cone scale with `reach`, so they
  stay inside the light. Owner: "yes".

Decided by the **owner**, 2026-09-29, after G1: **one lamp, no variant B.** He chose at G1 and
runs no bench comparison. The table ships his sliders, vigR 380 · poolR 380 · youY 380, as one
`LAMP_LIGHT`, and every bound is asserted on it. Task 9's bench measures that one light; Task 10
and the G2/G3 runs wait for his device pass after all plans land, and until then `LAMP_FLOOR`
stays the model's 3.8.

Departures ruled at Task 8, 2026-09-29 (never a moved bound):
- **A ring reads only felt.** "In the Skia felt's own pixels" means the rail and the room never
  enter a ring: `feltOnly` clears everything outside `ringRect(RAIL_BAND)` before
  `annulusLuminance` reads, so `RING_COVERAGE` counts felt pixels. A seat whose ring cannot reach
  95 % felt has the wrong ring shape for that seat; the coverage is not lowered.
- **The fallback's light does not sway.** The web fallback bakes a still felt, so the fallback rest
  parity runs both sides unswayed (the app under reduced motion, the mockup's `lamp.lx` held at
  `lamp.x`); the Skia parity specs keep their sway.
