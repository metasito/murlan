## Goal

The owner's findings 1 and 8 on #1259, measured in the research
(https://github.com/metasito/murlan/blob/claude/lantern-review-1259-efbf50/docs/research/2026-09-28-lantern-review.md,
§ "UI-thread and JS-thread load that is not audio"):
a tap lifts its card on the UI thread and re-renders only what shows the selection; a played
card is one view from throw to sweep; the felt draws every card's shadow in one Skia pass,
following the lamp (#1260's end state), so no table card keeps a masked platform shadow; the deal
flies on a pool of views; the felt is an opaque Metal layer only if the device shows it pays.

**Owner-visible outcome:** fast taps never lag; throws and the deal never hitch; shadows move
with the lamp as in The Lantern Table; the beaten play becomes the mockup's.
